THE FIRST WEEK

Learn it. Log it. Come back.

01

Make it your weight.

Start light. Aim to finish with about 3 good reps left. An empty bar can be too heavy: use bodyweight, a securely held light plate, or an easier variation.

Record total weight, including the bar, in pounds. Bodyweight means 0 lb added. Assisted chin-ups use 0 lb added; describe your assistance in the notes.

02

Warm up before working sets.

Walk easily for 5 minutes, then rehearse the day’s movements. Do 1–2 light practice sets before the first loaded exercises. Practice sets don’t count toward the two working sets.

03

Train side by side.

Switch profiles before logging. Alternate turns, change plates carefully, and rest 90–120 seconds between your own sets. Use rack safeties for squats and bench presses.

04

Keep cardio conversational.

On easy walks, you should be able to converse. Brisk intervals are controlled effort, about 6/10, without sprinting. If the treadmill slips, walk outdoors until it’s fixed.

05

Progress one step at a time.

Next week, keep the exercises and sets. Add reps within the target range first. When both sets reach the top comfortably, add the smallest available weight increase. Repeat a weight longer if the next jump is too large.

06

Adjust to the day.

Stop movements that cause sharp pain, instability, or worsening symptoms. If soreness changes how you move, shorten or lighten the session. The goal is to feel able to repeat the routine.
